Tesla Roadster "Rocket" — Flight & Highway Simulator

Modeled after the Next-Gen Tesla Roadster (Rocket Configuration) demonstrated on October 15, 2026 in Texas. Features fighter-jet inspired Ano White bodywork, a Gold Glazed astronaut-visor single-seat canopy, active Optimus-actuated rear wing, underbody suction fan skirt, and 10 SpaceX cold-gas supersonic thrusters fed by twin 600L Carbon-Overwrapped Pressure Vessels (COPVs) delivering 80+ kN of thrust.

1. Driving & Vertical Flight

  • W / H / B / S : W Accelerate, H Foot Brake, B Handbrake Drift, S Reverse
  • A / D : Steer Left / Right across dense 6-lane highway traffic
  • SPACE : Ignite vertical lift thrusters to Hover & Ascend
  • SHIFT : Fire retro-thrusters for controlled Descent

2. 6-DOF Mid-Air Attitude (RCS)

  • ← / → (Arrows) : Roll left / right (hold for 360° barrel rolls!)
  • ↑ / ↓ (Arrows) : Pitch nose up / down
  • Q / E : Yaw rotate nose left / right
  • O / R : Toggle RCS Auto-Stabilizer (`O`) or Reset Car (`R`)

🔊 Speed of Sound & Supersonic Gameplay Tuning

In reality, the speed of sound at sea level (Mach 1) is 343 m/s (1,235 km/h / 767 MPH). For gameplay excitement and visual impact, we adjusted the in-game supersonic Mach barrier to 370 km/h (230 MPH / 102.8 m/s)!

3. 10-Second COPV Compressed Air Tank Physics

• 0.0s – 8.0s Used (100% → 20% Air): Full supersonic lift and attitude control.
• At 8.0s Used (2.0s / 20% Air Remaining): Tank pressure drops below hover threshold — Auto-Descent engages automatically even if holding SPACE.
• At 10.0s Used (0% Air Remaining): Compressed air is exhausted — thrusters shut off and the Roadster enters immediate Freefall! Touch down on the highway to rapidly repressurize the 600L tanks.
