The native **Autopilot / 自动驾驶** switch is available only on Map 01, the six-lane elevated highway. It stays reachable in all three HUD modes. It has no keyboard shortcut, starts off, and is not persisted across reloads. Land before enabling it. Switch off to regain manual control.

## Behavior

The driver supplies analog inputs through the production input reader. Its opt-in ground-only stunt steering has a quicker bounded rack and lateral-velocity response; manual keyboard/mobile driving, city handling, drift and airborne handling retain their original parameters. Position still advances by real integrated velocity, with no teleport. It never writes the vehicle's speed, horizontal position, tank, score, collision result, or NPC behavior. In normal Realistic driving it aims for 220+ km/h, with a 250 km/h cruise target in traffic. Empty-road, Plaid and Fantasy propulsion retain their existing limits. Acceleration from rest and after congestion is physical; the speedometer is unchanged.

The planner chooses among all six final lane centers. A two-or-more-lane maneuver uses a single continuous steering target, without recentering, dwelling or making a new ordinary routing decision at an intermediate center. The same rack/yaw/lateral integrator predicts the complete transition. A final target remains committed until settled; a newly unsafe path can extend the move in the same direction instead of alternating steering directions. Normal manual steering and physics retain their existing response.

Braking follows the updated 220 km/h policy. The driver predicts the combined ordinary H mechanical brake and speed-dependent, paid reverse-jet impulse, including available gas and current frame time. It caps a brake request so the resulting speed stays at or above 220 km/h. A queue that would require slower following is a routing/jump request instead of permission to brake down to NPC speed. Below 220, including startup from zero, the driver accelerates physically. Ground-only floor recovery may temporarily override ordinary recharge hysteresis when real paid boost is available; a floor-protecting ground pulse is also included in the admitted recovery model, with its real burn and lost recharge extending the wait. It never assigns a minimum speed or replaces the speedometer value.

The planner considers actual NPC x/z, speed, observed acceleration, active lane-change motion, rear approaches, hidden/waiting spawns and low wreck motion. Each ground-lane candidate is predicted for up to 2.4 seconds; acceptance requires a full final-center arrival plus 0.25 seconds of clear hold. There is no fixed short prefix that can omit late intermediate-lane contacts. Collision margins remain narrow (0.18 m lateral beyond the existing contact envelope and 0.35 m longitudinal plus bounded prediction uncertainty), without shrinking actual contact bounds. Validation sweeps relative boxes continuously between predictor samples. Active demo ground movement also retains the existing real continuous frame-chord collision sweep and ordinary damage/destruction. Prediction is bounded, with no guarantee against every sudden or impossible configuration.

A finite plan includes the normal ground bridge, one to three paid hops, each complete ascent/arc/touchdown/rearm/refill phase, and a fully swept 2.4-second normal-ground terminal window. Search preserves the existing low-height/paired-action ordering, tries a safe one-hop terminal first, then backtracks B before changing A and only then considers C. It stops at a finite36³ candidate-node cap and an8-second projected-ground cursor. There is no recursive D proof or infinite flight horizon. A clear touchdown or recharge-to3.6 alone is not described as a safe continuation.

The wait uses the real3.4/s ground recharge,0.12-second rearm, discrete roof-clearance rise time and three fixed physics-step allowances. A necessary floor-protection ground jet pulse pays its actual0.85/s burn and forfeits that frame’s recharge. At every physical frame the live controller shares the same normal-ground decision/state transition used by projection. Already promised B/C still receive their own fresh, current-state full-arc checks; an unready later action never becomes an extra gate that prevents the existing promise.

Planning is split across renders with one shared192-work-unit counter and20ms measured deadline, including all jobs and up to three physical ticks. This count uses actual sweep work and is not equivalent to earlier96-unit budgets. One indivisible sweep/routing/physics step or GC can exceed20ms; this is not a hard whole-frame latency claim. No deadline miss synchronously drains a search. Missing/unready proof retains the normal actual inputs and restarts bounded planning, with real finite-fuel/collision limits still applying.

Only an exact source-pinned proof can be reused: every intervening audited control/physical field, ordered NPC numerical/metadata value, route/history, real input/ownership, fixed dt, schema/epoch and prepared launch checkpoint must match with Object.is and property-presence checks. Missing frames, signed-zero differences, runtime-source changes or mismatched actual landings revoke authority immediately. A-stage prefetch is only computation; it gains no authority before the actual fresh B+C commitment and complete exact bridge match. The actual C is still independently fresh-validated. Predicted or merely close endpoints remain ordering hints; no cached endpoint is copied over a live vehicle.

For each hop candidate, the ordinary NPC controller advances on separate copies of every highway car, including hidden/waiting entries. Each possible player trajectory has independent reservations and reactions. Respawns break the sweep segment and retain the new endpoint; the separate blast integrator is also predicted. No speculative call receives a live mesh, reservation or car object.

The driver first tries the existing low obstacle-clearance height, then 3.2/4.5/6/7.5/10 m only when a lower complete arc has no valid landing/preparation path. Every option has a finite real-gas budget and retains a positive low-pressure reserve. A selected landing lane and height stay locked for that hop. The vertical predictor follows the ordinary O ascent, Earth gravity and landing-save phases at the demo’s fixed1/60-second physical interval. Render-clock tests cover30/60/144Hz without changing ordinary manual animate(dt). Full low-altitude sweeps include body contact and the descending roof-rebound band. No gas, position, speed or collision state is assigned by the planner.

The shared O-jump action still performs each takeoff and pays its existing lift cost. Only a requested demo hop receives a bounded launch-vector grace matched to its planned finite arc plus 0.18 seconds, capped at 2.4 seconds; manual O retains its existing 0.25-second grace and six-metre target. Ground contact clears demo grace. There is no airborne throttle, rear boost, altitude hold or sustained lift request after launch. Paid lateral strafe follows the planned landing center. Jumps cannot be extended while airborne or replaced by perpetual forward-powered flight. Actual landing/NPC behavior remains bounded prediction rather than a proof against every future configuration.

The 220 floor constrains commanded braking; it is not a physical guarantee. A depleted/reserved tank can prevent a necessary boost or hop, long airborne/roof-rebound intervals still undergo ordinary drag, and real collisions can slow or destroy the vehicle. Ground recharge and every gas debit remain unchanged. No infinite fuel, teleporting, forced speed or collision immunity is introduced.

## Demo simulation clock

While this switch owns input, simulation uses fixed1/60-second steps, at most three per render. Short time debt is retained. If debt exceeds0.15 seconds during sustained below20fps operation, the demo explicitly stops and restores manual ownership instead of silently dropping simulation time while claiming the same real-time220km/h behavior. Stopping, menus and input ownership are checked before every additional tick. Ordinary manual render-dt simulation is unchanged.

## Input and lifecycle

The switch owns driving inputs while active or preparing; held keyboard, legacy hold buttons and mobile sticks cannot inject a competing drive command. Stopping clears both virtual and manual held inputs. Camera, HUD, language and ordinary browser shortcuts remain usable. Reset, destruction, map/mode/traffic changes, opening map/help/leaderboard/audio/paint menus, blur, hidden and page exit stop the demo, without automatically restarting it on return.

## Ranked isolation

Enabling first invalidates any starting/running 60-second challenge and awaits its exact server cancellation, including an issuance that arrives late. An unconfirmed end fails closed and retains that run ID for retry. The demo cannot start a ranked run, including a programmatic disabled-button dispatch. It never creates or submits an automatic result. A previously completed immutable manual result remains its original manual snapshot and keeps the ordinary Submit/Discard flow before another challenge. Switching off allows a fresh manual challenge after pending ends are confirmed; there is no intentional 65-second local lock.
