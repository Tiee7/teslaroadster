## Conclusion

**No outstanding implementation blocker found for this bounded, unranked highway demonstration**, with the measured speed and adverse-fixture limits below. This conclusion covers the whole feature and its real input/physics/ranked integration, not only the final scoring delta. It does not assert continuous 220+ driving, optimal route selection or universal crash avoidance. Production was not edited in this review.

## Scope and resolved findings

- **UI and ownership:** native highway-only switch, no new shortcut, translated state/ARIA, full/medium/minimal HUD reachability. Active and preparing states own drive and attitude readers; keyboard, direct key injection, legacy hold controls and mobile sticks cannot compete. Stop clears captured/held inputs. Focused-button Space preserves native activation without injecting lift; modifier browser defaults are not swallowed. Fake-DOM coverage checks dispatch/default handling, not physical device hit testing.
- **Real propulsion and gas:** planner emits ordinary inputs; it does not assign speed/position/tank/score or suppress collisions. Throttle/rear boost and H braking are mutually exclusive. Existing mechanical H, paid high-speed reverse jets, mass, empty/freefall behavior, fractional last-drop impulse and bounded ground recharge are retained. The predicted retro debit previously overstated empty/freefall/fractional-gas braking; this was corrected and compared with real production motion. Manual W/H priority remains unchanged outside demo.
- **Traffic, routes and centering:** both front and rear approaches participate in collision prediction, with the highway longitudinal scale, observed NPC acceleration, active lane-change motion and hidden/waiting exclusions. Earlier competing lateral paths were replaced by one integrated steering/yaw/velocity trajectory for gap validation, route ranking and emergency checks. A selected multi-stage route formerly lost its tail; it now retains and revalidates adjacent-center stages. Targets are whole lane centers, with arrival settling before the next stage. Prediction remains bounded and cannot foresee every future NPC choice.
- **Following and short hop:** approved temporary slowing uses the same leader/control model in prediction and execution, then releases braking when a gap clears. Comfort following is separate from physical emergency margins, avoiding unnecessary hops. The old near-ground test could re-launch before actual landing; true ground contact is now required. Emergency hops use the existing O action with a lower clearance target and bounded demo-only launch grace. There is no ongoing airborne throttle/rear boost/hover request; launch lift and optional lateral correction still incur real gas. Normal manual O remains its original action.
- **Ranked and lifecycle:** starting/running challenges are invalidated before activation. Late issuance is released by exact run ID; unconfirmed end fails closed and remains retryable, with shared pending requests and versioned cancellation. Programmatic ranked start is guarded during preparation/active demo; demo scores are never submitted. A prior completed manual snapshot stays immutable. Exit permits a fresh ordinary challenge after ends are confirmed. Blur/hidden/pagehide, reset/destruction, map/mode/traffic changes and map/help/ranked/audio/paint overlays stop without automatic resume. The missing paint-menu stop and preparation-only analog leak were found and fixed.
