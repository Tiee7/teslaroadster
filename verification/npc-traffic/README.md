# Historical models reused by random NPC traffic

This clean, separately versioned runtime library reuses the visual shapes of all
20 preserved reference-model entries (12 different geometric versions), together
with the prior classic Roadster and sculpted touring/track traffic styles.
Identical rebases, normal-only revisions and appearance-only captures retain
separate provenance identities without pretending to be different shapes.

## Source boundary

- Base: current released `a9690717613d92441c5eefffe65207c954d35b54`.
- No player body, cockpit, control, audio, speed, passing or collision changes.
- Every historical entry maps through immutable `catalog-v3.json` to a live NPC
  style; each NPC stores both its source entry and its geometric style identity.
- All old archive objects/catalogs remain unchanged. Known failed clustering
  derivatives are neither read by the clean generator nor shipped by the game.
- Hidden FPV pods, old unnamed projecting instrument housings, cabin UI and
  interior controls are omitted from traffic rather than forced visible.

## Geometry

`build-assets.mjs` reads exact archived preview geometry and applies a new,
parameter-topology-aware retopology. It verifies rectangular surface topology,
selects sparse interior control vertices, and triangulates each cell while
retaining every original panel-boundary vertex at near detail. No independent
per-panel vertex clustering is used. Existing planar details and non-grid
front-shoulder transitions keep their source geometry. The far tier removes
subpixel trim and reduces boundary density.

Source transforms are baked with negative-determinant correction. Culling is
validated after final transport quantization: 0.1 mm positions, signed 8-bit
normals renormalized on decode, and 16-bit indices. Degenerate source slivers are
removed and triangle winding is aligned with authored outward normals. Opaque
interior backing and the historical closed diffuser prevent see-through tails.

Four closed, circular symmetric inset wheel discs replace heavy brake/spoke
assemblies. NPC wheels have a static neutral pose, with no spin animation or
per-frame wheel work. Player wheels and animation are untouched.

## Runtime budgets and lifetime

- 15 different geometric styles, selected randomly once per population spawn.
- 20 historical entry identities plus explicit legacy-style source references.
- Geometry/paint identity stays fixed through movement, road wraps and LOD swaps.
- Six opaque, shared-material batches per car; no extra shadow casters/receivers.
- At most 52 NPCs; at most the nearest eight within 64 m use the near tier.
- Near is below 19,000 triangles; far is below 3,500 triangles per car.
- One bounded geometry cache for the fixed asset inventory; repeated population
  changes remove instance nodes without disposing a sibling's shared geometry.
- Six role materials and eight paint materials are globally shared. No material
  clones, texture copies, runtime mesh decimation or per-frame geometry allocation.
- Every frame uses a fixed eight-entry scratch buffer. Slots are cleared after
  the update, so removed populations are not retained through that buffer.

The generated JS is approximately 3.7 MB before compression / 1.7 MB gzip. It
ships only compact clean profiles, never the separately versioned original archive or
its quarantined payloads. Triangle/draw budgets are CPU/scene-graph evidence;
this offline check does not claim measured browser GPU frame time or FPS.

## Reproduce and verify

First recover the separately versioned ZIP named in assets/vehicle-variants/README.md to /tmp/recovered. Then run from the repository root using the official Three.js r128 runtime:

    ROADSTER_ARCHIVE_ROOT=/tmp/recovered/assets/vehicle-variants node verification/npc-traffic/build-assets.mjs /tmp/roadster-three.min.js
    node verification/npc-traffic/runtime-check.cjs /tmp/roadster-three.min.js
    node verification/npc-traffic/export-preview.cjs
    python3 verification/npc-traffic/render-all.py
    node --test tests/npc-traffic-assets.test.mjs
    python3 bundle_app.py
    npm run check
    npm run build

Actual renders use Blender Cycles CPU with the exported live packed geometry and
FrontSide material culling. Studio floor, lighting and camera are presentation
only. No model images are generated by an image model. Raw exports and renders
are local review products; the compact contact sheets and numeric render audit
are retained alongside this document.

## Final legacy-only quality repair

The three older low-resolution source grids need conservative sampling. Their
canopies must not use the generic far step. The sculpted touring/track far tier
shares its verified near canopy; the classic uses a denser canopy and hood grid.
Classic white roof bands partition the glass triangles into disjoint material
regions, with no offset overlay or floating source strips.

Final legacy budgets are 2,966 / 1,970 triangles (classic near/far) and 3,710 /
3,434 triangles (track and touring). The global fixed cache is 85 geometries and
14 shared materials. The eight-near-car and zero-shadow-draw limits stay intact.

`historical-style-baseline.json` and `legacy-lod-fix-scope.json` verify that all
packed batches of the other 12 historical geometric styles remain byte-identical.
Their 72 earlier CPU views are retained with original source hashes and explicit
mesh/material equivalence evidence. The three repaired styles have 18 fresh CPU
views. `render-result.json` records the exact original and live payload hashes;
a reused image is never relabeled as a newly rendered payload.

The original ZIP and SHA manifest are complete and retained locally. Publication
of the dedicated archive branch is pending tool approval. The initial whole-ZIP
upload exceeded the tool gateway's 16 MiB request limit and returned no blob SHA;
no remote tree, commit, branch or main update was made by this task.

## Serial traffic/scoring integration

The independent integration branch layers traffic/scoring input `631dd073` after
NPC candidate `4141bf0`, while retaining both source branches. Bundler and taxi
assertion conflicts keep driving-score immediately after config, highway traffic
before vehicles, NPC data before its factory, the distance-score reset, and the
visual LOD hook before rendering.

Legacy provenance fingerprints now use a stable `position-topology-pose-v1`
content hash. Random Three.js UUIDs, material IDs, names and normals cannot change
that hash. Two separate complete asset builds produce identical payload bytes.
All 85 packed geometry records and every 15-style near/far reference are exactly
unchanged from the already CPU-reviewed model. `metadata-fingerprint-equivalence`
records why those existing actual renders remain applicable after the metadata
repair. No player visual candidate is included.

New integrated tests execute the actual NPC factory and actual traffic controller
for 20/36/52 pools at 30 Hz. They preserve speed-band targets, model/source identity
and LOD limits, check every active NPC pair, and assert queued-to-spawn cars remain
hidden. Hidden offscreen waiting placeholders are intentionally excluded from the
active collision check.
